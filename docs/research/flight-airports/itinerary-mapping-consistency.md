## Method and limitations

The actual `shortbreakhub-prerender-data.detail` JSON was read from every `dist/itinerary/<slug>/index.html`, associated by `hotelDestination.destinationKey` with the canonical catalogue. Every nested text field was inspected—not only `arrival`—including schedule, tips, practical advice, gettingAround and transport notes. Airport codes and identifiable airport-name aliases were extracted with field paths. Hotel metadata, image paths and generic transit acronyms were excluded. Arrival options were distinguished from departure-only references. Raw quote wording is retained; its durations, frequencies and onward claims are product text, not newly verified transport facts.

Some arrival notes contain a missing name (for example “arrive via ,”) in the actual data. The airport code can still establish a match if present. Without an explicit identifiable name/code, the classification remains INSUFFICIENT_INFORMATION rather than guessing from the destination or the mapping. Three canonical entries (San Miguel de Allende, Arequipa, Washington, D.C.) have no actual itinerary detail in this snapshot. They remain in the 201-row review; their approved flight entry mapping does not fabricate itinerary content.

MATCH indicates consistency of named arrival options, not revalidation of timetable/transport claims. COMPATIBLE_GATEWAY means the arrival airports differ but serve different declared purposes; it does not promise convenient onward travel. Myanmar status stays blocked regardless of text consistency. Historical local-airport candidates in research are not substituted into itinerary copy.

## Confirmed conflicts after the two corrections

**None remain.** Phnom Penh now classifies as MATCH; Palawan now classifies as COMPATIBLE_GATEWAY because ENI/USU are local arrivals while MNL remains the approved country-entry gateway.

Primary evidence for the specific Palawan factual contradiction: [Philippines aviation authority report, aerodrome information](https://caap.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Serious-Incident-RP-C7202-1.pdf) identifies Lio/El Nido as **ENI**; [Cebu Pacific airport directory](https://cebupacific-hc.lighthouse-cloud.com/article/cebu-pacific-destinations-airports-and-terminal-numbers-120302) also identifies ENI and current terminal caveats. These sources do not validate the itinerary’s transfer durations. Phnom Penh KTI replacement evidence is preserved in the already approved `cambodia--phnom-penh` research record; no new broad research was performed.

## Minimal fixes by responsible data layer

- **Completed frontend corrections:** Phnom Penh’s legacy arrival title now identifies Techo International Airport (KTI), with transport wording supported by the already approved taxi/ride-hailing/Airport Express evidence. Old PNH-specific transfer timing is not carried over. Palawan’s explicit IATA label is ENI; the El Nido/Coron transfer and destination guidance is otherwise unchanged. These exact slug-scoped corrections apply to live transport projection and prerender detail, without mutating API input. Backend editorial content is unchanged. No confirmed CONFLICT remains.
- **Airport mapping:** no automatic corrections. Retain all 130 prior approvals. A different itinerary arrival option alone does not invalidate a VERIFIED mapping. Existing local alternatives worth owner review include Ipoh IPH, Sukhothai THS, Rotorua ROT, Hue HUI, and Palermo PMO; the current country gateways remain allowed by policy. Existing evidence supports some local choices more strongly than others; this report does not grant new verification.
- **Future booking presentation:** any country fallback must be described as a country flight-search entry point, not “the airport for this itinerary,” “nearby airport,” or arrival in the destination. Keep the itinerary’s actual arrival instructions visible and distinguish onward flights, ferries and transfers. No affiliate builder or UI changes are included.

## Gateway cases that can mislead without the product distinction

The following approved country fallbacks particularly require clear wording: BSB for Manaus or Atins; SCL for Torres del Paine or San Pedro; FCO for Sicily; MNL for Palawan; WLG for Rotorua; JNB for Cape Town; DAR for northern Tanzania; LIM for Machu Picchu/Sacred Valley; LHR for the Lake District. These are country entry points and are not interchangeable with local arrival. The report flags the risk without overriding the owner-approved policy. All 11 previously classified COMPATIBLE_GATEWAY itinerary records and all 95 INSUFFICIENT_INFORMATION itinerary records are unchanged; Palawan moves from CONFLICT to COMPATIBLE_GATEWAY after its IATA correction.

All 60 new country fallbacks and their reasons are recorded in [country-gateway-fallback.csv](country-gateway-fallback.csv); exact local recommendations, when available, appear below.
