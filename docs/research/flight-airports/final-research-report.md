## Evidence boundaries

The local-passenger-first policy permits practical domestic connections; a distant international hub is not automatically the destination airport. DIRECT describes geographical service, not a nonstop flight. Existing actual itinerary arrival/schedule/transport context was reviewed before targeted new searches. Prior approved airport-operating evidence was reused only for the same physical airport and explicitly attributed.

New observations state exactly what primary indexed content, retrieved page or PDF established. `PRIMARY_INDEXED_CONTENT_REVIEW` records indexed excerpts rather than claiming full page inspection; historical source leads without an observation are not verification. The XIY government T5 page timed out on full retrieval; indexed government opening evidence, municipal metro interchange guidance and operating-airline terminal guidance corroborate the airport access. An inaccessible page never proves an airport unsuitable. Physical IATA identities retain the existing reference inventory with airport/airline corroboration for promotions. No flight frequency, transfer duration, fare, live Trip.com inventory or guaranteed year-round route is invented.

REVIEW_REQUIRED means the precise recorded gap remains; it is not an empty airport code or a substituted capital fallback. UNAVAILABLE was not used merely for insufficient evidence, safety uncertainty or an unspecified endpoint. A static mapping is insufficient for itinerary-specific island/lodge choices when the entry endpoint cannot be established.
